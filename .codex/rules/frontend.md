# Frontend Rules

## Product feel

- Build Delphi as an internal operational tool.
- Optimize for scanability, density, and repeated use.
- Avoid marketing layouts, oversized hero treatment, and decorative filler.

## Information architecture

- Lead with the dataset overview and open violation posture.
- Treat the dataset detail page as the main working surface.
- Keep essential tabs close to the data:
  - Overview
  - Current Contract
  - Contract History
  - Evaluations
  - Violations
  - Ask Delphi

## Interaction principles

- Every health state should be explainable from visible evidence.
- Prefer tables, compact summaries, segmented controls, and clear status chips.
- Make evaluation triggering obvious and fast.
- Keep contract diffs and history easy to compare.
- Show evidence with NL answers, not just prose.

## Visual guidance

- Use restrained styling and compact spacing.
- Favor utility over ornament.
- Severity and status should be distinguishable at a glance.
- Avoid nested cards and avoid turning every page section into a card.
